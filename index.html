Frequently Asked Questions

Which tracking parameters are stripped?

Over 40 tracking tokens are purged, including all utm_* tags, Google click identifiers (gclid, gbraid, wbraid), Meta tags (fbclid, igshid), Twitter (twclid), TikTok (ttclid), YouTube share IDs (si), and Amazon referral bloat.

Will YouTube or Amazon product links still work?

Yes. The video ID (v=...) and Amazon product ASIN are preserved. Only surveillance markers (such as session tracking si=... and ref=...) are excised.

Can I clean links inside an entire email or markdown document?

Yes. Switch to Batch Article / Text mode to paste an entire document. Every URL inside will be sanitized automatically without altering the surrounding text.

Does this tool work completely offline?

Yes. All logic is self-contained and operates fully without an active internet connection.
